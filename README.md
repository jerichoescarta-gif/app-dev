# Stranger Things

> *"Friends don't lie."* — Eleven  

---

## Overview

**Stranger Things** is a *science fiction horror series* created by the **Duffer Brothers**.  
It takes place in the 1980s in the small town of **Hawkins, Indiana**, where a group of kids encounters mysterious experiments, supernatural forces, and a strange girl with powers.

---

## Main Characters

| Character | Actor | Description |
|------------|--------|-------------|
| Eleven | Millie Bobby Brown | A girl with telekinetic powers |
| Mike Wheeler | Finn Wolfhard | Eleven’s best friend |
| Dustin Henderson | Gaten Matarazzo | The funny and smart one |
| Lucas Sinclair | Caleb McLaughlin | The brave friend |
| Jim Hopper | David Harbour | The town’s police chief |
| Joyce Byers | Winona Ryder | Will’s determined mother |

---

## Seasons Overview

1. **Season 1** – The disappearance of Will Byers and the discovery of the Upside Down  
2. **Season 2** – The aftermath of Will’s return and Eleven’s new life  
3. **Season 3** – The group faces a new threat at the Starcourt Mall  
4. **Season 4** – Vecna, a terrifying new villain, emerges from the Upside Down  

---

## Why I Love It

- It gives a strong *80s nostalgia* vibe  
- The friendship between the characters is heartwarming  
- The mix of mystery, horror, and adventure keeps it exciting  
- It has great music and emotional moments
